## Magic Tower: Key of Fate — Data Deletion

**App**: Magic Tower: Key of Fate  **Developer**: Sublevel Games

### How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **sublevelgames@gmail.com**
2. Subject: **Magic Tower Data Deletion Request**
3. Include your in-game **nickname** and the **flag** you selected, so we can find your record.
   If you also remember your best floor in The Descent, please add it — it helps us match the right account.

We process requests within **7 business days** and confirm by email.

### What is deleted

| Data | Where | On request |
|---|---|---|
| Nickname, country flag, platform | Cloud profile | **Deleted** |
| Leaderboard entry (best floor, kills, nickname, flag, character) | Public leaderboard | **Deleted** |
| Cloud save (progress, unlocked characters, currency, quests) | Cloud save | **Deleted** |
| Anonymous sign-in identifier | Authentication | **Deleted** |
| Purchase receipts | Google Play | Retained by Google — see Google's policy. We keep only a transaction reference for up to **5 years** where tax law requires it. |
| Advertising and analytics events (AdMob, GameAnalytics) | Third-party services | Not linked to your nickname. Reset or delete your Android advertising ID in **Settings → Privacy → Ads** to stop further association. |

Deleting data on our servers does not remove the game's local save on your device. Uninstalling the app, or using **Reset Progress** in the game's settings, clears that.
