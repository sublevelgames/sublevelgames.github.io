## Magic Tower: Key of Fate — Pre-Registration Reward Terms and Conditions

**App**: Magic Tower: Key of Fate  **Developer**: Sublevel Games
**Last updated**: September 16, 2026

These terms govern the pre-registration reward offered for *Magic Tower: Key of Fate*
("the Game") through Google Play. By pre-registering for the Game you agree to these terms.

### 1. The reward

Users who pre-register for the Game on Google Play and then install and launch it receive,
once per Google account, the **Founder's Pack**:

- **150 Emeralds**
- **1,000 Silver**

Emeralds and Silver are in-game currencies with no monetary value. They cannot be exchanged
for cash, transferred between accounts, or redeemed outside the Game.

### 2. Who is eligible

- You must pre-register for the Game on Google Play before the Game is released.
- You must install and launch the Game using the **same Google account** you pre-registered with.
- The reward is granted **once per Google account**, regardless of how many devices you use.
- The reward is available in every country where the Game is offered on Google Play.

### 3. When and how you receive it

The reward is delivered by Google Play when you first launch the Game after release.
Delivery is automatic — there is no code to enter. The Game shows an in-game message
confirming the reward once it arrives.

Delivery may take a few minutes after your first launch. If the reward has not appeared,
launching the Game again while connected to the internet will retry delivery. Your progress
is stored per account, so the reward follows your account if you reinstall or change devices.

### 4. Limits

- The reward has no cash value and is not refundable, transferable, or exchangeable.
- The reward cannot be combined with, or converted into, any paid in-app product.
- The reward is separate from any in-app purchase. Pre-registering costs nothing and does
  not obligate you to buy anything.
- We may not be able to deliver the reward if your Google Play account is suspended, if the
  Game is unavailable in your country, or if you pre-registered with a different account
  from the one you install with.

### 5. Changes and cancellation

We may withdraw or change the pre-registration campaign before the Game is released, subject
to Google Play policies. If we do, we will honour the reward for everyone who pre-registered
before the change. We may update these terms to correct errors or to comply with applicable
law or Google Play policies; the current version is always the one published on this page.

### 6. Privacy

The reward is delivered through Google Play using your Google account. We do not receive
your name, email address, or payment details. See our
[Privacy Policy](https://sublevelgames.github.io/privacy_policy/) for how the Game handles data.
