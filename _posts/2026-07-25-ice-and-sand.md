This is a Playgama AFK Summer Jam 2026 submission.

Summer is the worst possible season to build a castle out of ice. Build one anyway.

Every level shows you a target silhouette. Drop ice pieces onto the beach to match it — but here's the catch: every piece you place costs one sun pass. Any ice exposed to the sky melts away, and ice with nothing beneath it falls. The castle you're building is also the castle that's disappearing.
