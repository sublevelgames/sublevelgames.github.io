Track Puzzle is a relaxing logic puzzle about building railways. Each row and column shows how many track cells it holds. Use the numbers to work out where the rails go, then lay one continuous track from Start to Goal.

Along the way, pick up gems and deliver them to houses of the same color. Plan the order carefully, because your wagons can only carry so much!

Over 300 levels slowly introduce new ideas: crossings, tunnels, switches and gates, one-way tiles, bigger houses and more wagons. Solve the Daily Puzzle, take on huge Big Maps, and race 100 players in the Train Rally. Collect new trains and smoke effects, earn awards, and climb the leaderboard. Easy to learn, hard to master!
