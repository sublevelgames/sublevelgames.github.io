Ice Hop is a fast-paced ice-hopping arcade game where you guide a penguin across drifting ice floes, timing every leap to stay out of the water. Dodge flying fish and diving seagulls, hop onto ice tiles to collect coins, and rescue baby penguins along the way. Between runs, spend your coins on the gacha for new characters, complete daily quests, and chase a spot on the global leaderboard — what starts as a simple hop-or-fall challenge quickly turns into a race against your own best score.
