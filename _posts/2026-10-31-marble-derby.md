Marble Derby is a vertical marble race for your phone. Pick a candy-glass marble and race it down a twisting course against up to 7 rivals. You don’t just watch: tap when the shrinking ring hits your marble to dash, or swipe to fling it in any direction. Land a PERFECT for a huge burst of speed, smash rivals out of the way, ride boost pads, and break through worn pegs on your way to the finish.

How to play
- Tap when the ring closes on your marble to dash (PERFECT / GOOD / MISS)
- Swipe to dash in that direction
- Dashes use charges that refill over time
- Win SHORT to unlock MEDIUM, and MEDIUM to unlock LONG

Features
- 3 courses: Short (4 marbles), Medium (6), Long (8)
- 8 marbles, each with its own weight and bounce
- A new procedurally generated track every race
- 13 achievements, stats, and a quick tutorial
- 10 languages
