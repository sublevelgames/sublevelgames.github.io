NEON BREAKER is a vertical arcade space shooter with a roguelite heart, drawn in
glowing vector neon straight out of a classic arcade monitor.

Blast your way through 45 sectors of splitting asteroids. Every run starts fresh:
level up mid-battle and build your loadout from 35+ upgrade cards — extra barrels,
homing rockets, lasers, chain lightning, mines, drones, shields and more. Cards
stack and multiply, so no two runs play the same.

Between runs, spend salvaged credits in the Armory on permanent ship upgrades,
and take on side trials: clear the Firing Range to raise your stat caps, survive
the Evasion Trial to unlock extra card slots, and beat the Boss Rush with a
loadout you pick yourself for a permanent bounty bonus.

Four boss archetypes — Core Sentinel, Swarm Mother, Beam Warden and Splitter
Prime — return in stronger MK versions as you push deeper. Grab Hull Repair,
Time Dilation and Overcharge drops mid-fight, chase your best sector on the
global leaderboard, and enjoy an original synth soundtrack with optional CRT
filter and neon glow you can toggle any time.

One more sector. You know you want to.
