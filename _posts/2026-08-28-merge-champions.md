Merge Champions is a merge auto-battler. Buy soldiers onto your grid, drag matching units together to merge them into stronger tiers, then send your army out and watch the battle play itself out. Two unit lines — melee and ranged, 11 tiers each — mean every run builds a different army. Beyond the main stages you unlock wave-survival dungeons, a timed Golden Goblin gold mine, and Lava Grounds PvP where you raid other players' armies for trophies.
