Janggi is Korean chess: chariots, cannons that jump, horses that can be blocked, and a general that never leaves the palace. Janggi 9 Dan takes you from your very first move to master level.

Climb the ranks. Start at 18 kyu and play your way up to 9 dan against nine local masters, from the old man in the park pavilion to the grandmaster on the championship stage. Every master has a personality, a home and a style of play, and each rank step is a real game against an AI tuned to that strength.

Learn from zero. Never played before? Baekgu the school dog teaches how every piece moves, one star at a time. Then guest teachers walk you through openings, tactics (forks, skewers, blocked horses, sacrifices) and endgame mates, with arrows and highlights on the board.

Solve puzzles. Books of checkmate puzzles from mate-in-1 upward, plus a daily puzzle. Get a wrong check and you see exactly how the general escapes.

Handicap games. Not ready for a full game? Play with the master missing a chariot, a cannon or more, and earn your way to even games.

Review every game. Your recent games are saved. Replay them move by move with an accuracy graph and the best move at each turn.

Make it yours. Collect coins and unlock boards, piece sets, profile frames and badges. Show off your rank in the Hall of Fame.

- 9 masters, 27 ranks from 18 kyu to 9 dan
- 29 lessons for beginners, with step-by-step hints
- Checkmate puzzle books and a daily puzzle
- Handicap games, game review with analysis, Hall of Fame
- Plays in 11 languages, on desktop and mobile
