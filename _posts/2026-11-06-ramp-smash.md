Ramp Smash is a one-shot physics puzzle. Little critters are frozen inside ice blocks, and all you have is a handful of marbles. Pick a spot on the rail, drop a marble, and watch it roll, bounce and ricochet through ramps, pegs and gadgets. Hit the ice hard enough and it shatters. Free every critter to clear the level, and use as few marbles as you can for three stars.

How to play
- Tap a spot on the rail at the top to drop a marble
- Tap a jelly ramp to flip which way it tilts
- Pick a marble from the tray at the bottom: some levels give you several kinds
- Break every ice block with a critter inside to clear the level
- Fewer marbles means more stars

Features
- 102 levels, with a new gadget every few stages
- 5 marble types: normal, heavy (smashes metal), bomb, and 2-way / 3-way splitters
- Gadgets: flip ramps, metal blocks, thick ice, bumpers, TNT, wooden bars that burn, springs, warp holes, moving platforms and cages
- Coins from every block you break, to spend on hints
- 12 languages (English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Turkish)
