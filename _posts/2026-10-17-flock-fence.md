Night is coming, and the wolves are hungry. Help the sheepdog guard the flock in Flock Fence, a cozy logic puzzle about drawing the perfect pen.

Tap the grass to put down fences and close off a pen around your sheepdog. Every tile inside the pen scores a point, and every sheep you keep safe scores extra. But you only have so many fences, and the wolves need their share. On some nights you’ll have to leave a few sheep out in the forest on purpose. Who do you protect, and who do you let go?

When the sun sets, the wolves come out of the forest. Leave even one gap and they’ll sneak into the pen!

Features
- 150 handcrafted levels, each with a best possible score to chase for 3 stars
- New twists keep coming: golden sheep, black sheep the wolves won’t touch, quarreling rams, mother and lamb pairs, wolf dens, muddy ground, thorny bushes, and a second sheepdog
- A new Daily Puzzle every day: the target is hidden and you only get one sunset, so make it count
- Spend your stars to build up your farm across 10 areas, from the dog yard to the market
- Collect 20 cute outfits for your sheep and dog in the Wardrobe
- Join the Sheepdog Trials: clear 5 levels in a row with 3 stars and share the prize
- Stuck? Hints show you one fence from the best answer, and you still get 3 stars
