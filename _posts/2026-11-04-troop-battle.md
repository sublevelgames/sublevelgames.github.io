Troop Battle is a bite-sized real-time tactics game for your phone. Your army is in retreat, home is ten stages away, and the undead block the road. Place squads on the grid, merge identical cards into stronger squads, and send them into the fight. Every soldier is simulated: each enemy pack has its own morale, so flanks and charges can rout one pack and drag its neighbours down with it. Your soldiers tire too, your Command limits how many squads you can field, and every loss carries over to the next stage.

How to play
- Drag squad cards onto the grid to deploy them
- Drop a card on an identical squad to raise its stars
- Hit FIGHT, then tap a squad to give orders: Advance, Charge, Guard or Fall Back
- Win a stage, pick 1 of 3 reinforcements, and march on
- Level up every 3 stages for more squads and more Command

Features
- 4 troop types: Spearmen, Axemen, Archers and Mages
- Pack-by-pack morale with cascading routs
- Stamina: exhausted soldiers drop for a moment, so rotate your squads
- A boss at stage 10, then reach home or go back for the comrades left behind
- Online leaderboard
- 2 languages (English, Korean)
